A message from your class

Words that don't
fit in a group chat

Ma'am Marissa — you walked into our classroom and turned a subject that can feel like pure intimidation into something we actually wanted to understand. You never made us feel slow for asking questions. You never made us feel like the board was talking at us instead of to us.

ICT became real in your hands — not just theory and acronyms, but things that actually connect to the world outside the gate. You gave us patience when the concept didn't click on the first try, and honesty when we needed to work harder. That combination is rarer than you think.

You show up every session with your whole self, and that matters more than we probably say out loud. Today we're saying it loud.

Thank you for being our teacher. Thank you for choosing this work. We see the effort behind every lesson, and we will carry what you've taught us well past Grade 11.
